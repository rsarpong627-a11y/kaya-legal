Delete Your Account

How to request account and data deletion

1 How to Request Deletion

To delete your Kaya account and all associated personal data, send an email to our support team with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

Please send the request from the email address linked to your Kaya account so we can verify your identity.

Email support@kayafast.com

2 What Gets Deleted

Upon a confirmed deletion request, the following data will be permanently removed within 30 days:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Your saved delivery addresses
  • Your device push notification tokens
  • Your profile photo (if uploaded)
  • Your order history and preferences

3 What May Be Retained

Some data may be retained beyond your deletion request where required by law or for legitimate business purposes.

The following may be kept for up to 7 years in accordance with Ghanaian financial regulations:

  • Transaction records and payment history (required for tax and financial compliance)
  • Order records where a dispute or refund is pending

This data is retained solely for legal compliance and is not used for marketing or shared with third parties.

4 Response Time

We will acknowledge your request within 48 hours and complete the deletion within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation email once your account has been fully deleted.
